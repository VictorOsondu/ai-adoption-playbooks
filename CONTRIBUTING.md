# Contributing

This is the index for the series. Most contributions belong in the individual playbook repos, not here — but a few things are welcome at the index level.

## Here (the index)

- **Corrections** to the series overview, links, or the [Playbook Standard](PLAYBOOK-STANDARD.md).
- **Suggestions for new playbooks** — an audience or setting the series doesn't yet cover. Open an issue describing who it's for and why the existing playbooks don't serve them.

## In the individual repos

Each playbook has its own `CONTRIBUTING.md` with rules specific to it. In general:

- Playbooks welcome corrections, real case studies, and translations.
- Reference repos welcome entries judged against a published bar.
- `resources/` sections welcome vetted links in the strict format set out in the [Playbook Standard](PLAYBOOK-STANDARD.md).

## Standards

Plain, specific, UK English. No hype. Curation means saying no more than yes.
