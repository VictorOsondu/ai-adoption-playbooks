## The idea

Most AI advice is either hype or homework — breathless threads, or a link to a two-hour webinar nobody finishes. These playbooks are neither. Each one is a short, specific guide to adopting AI in a particular setting, written from real delivery and public sources, and stripped of the noise.

They're deliberately **not** an awesome-list of links. The authored methodology leads; curated resources sit underneath it, never in front.

## The spine: two things every playbook shares

**1. The six-stage model.** Every playbook uses the same reflective model for *where a person or team may be stuck* — sorted by internal experience, not tools deployed:

> Nomad → Observer → Dabbler → Integrator → **Architect** → Innovator

The full model is its own canonical repo: **`ai-adoption-model`** (a local launch candidate, see the roster below).

**2. The ICE Method.** How to actually spend your AI effort, three ways:

- **Improve** — do work you already do, faster and better.
- **Create** — make things you couldn't make before.
- **Educate** — build the understanding to use AI well and know when not to.

## The house standard

These aren't awesome-lists, so they follow their own standard instead of a
borrowed one. Every playbook in the series has the same shape and the same
README structure — that consistency is what makes separate repos read as one
deliberate body of work. See **[PLAYBOOK-STANDARD.md](PLAYBOOK-STANDARD.md)**.

## Provenance

Anyone can collect links. The value here is authored judgement and honest labelling of where each claim comes from.

- **Practitioner-built** means drawn from delivery experience and adapted into reusable public guidance.
- **Sourced** means claims, examples, or risk guidance are anchored to public references.
- **Field-tested** means the methodology was used in a real setting and the repo says where that claim comes from.
- **Lean** means useful now, but still awaiting more field reports, examples, or audience-specific depth.

No invented provenance. If a playbook has not been field-tested, it should say so plainly.

External organisational playbooks are treated as reported practitioner cases. Useful operating patterns may be adapted with attribution, but company-specific mandates, metrics, terminology, and claims are not imported automatically. See the [source adaptation standard](PLAYBOOK-STANDARD.md#source-adaptation-standard).
